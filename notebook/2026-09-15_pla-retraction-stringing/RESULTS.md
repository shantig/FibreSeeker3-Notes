Physical sample **X0005** (formerly SMP-0005) (`experiments/Samples/samples.jsonl`; no tag).

`EXPERIMENTAL`. Follows the 2026-09-14 calibration
(`../2026-09-14-T1/RESULTS.md`), which found stringing present at **every**
band of the 220→195 °C temperature tower and therefore
temperature-independent. This session tests retraction.

Provenance: live Moonraker session under the owner's 2026-09-14 full-access
authorization. Machine left in `standby`, heaters off.

## Hypothesis

The Loom OrcaSlicer profile (`Loom P1 0.4 nozzle`) retracts **0.5 mm at
15 mm/s with wipe off** on a **direct-drive** extruder. `INFERENCE`: both the
length and the speed are low for direct drive, which fits stringing that does
not respond to temperature.

## Method — banded retraction test

A single print sweeps retraction while everything else is held at the
2026-09-14 accepted baseline (205 °C, first layer 210 °C, bed 55 °C, 0.2 mm
layers, right nozzle T1, right-nozzle Z offset 2.57, fresh full-bed mesh).

Model: two 8×8×30 mm pillars 60 mm apart (52 mm of travel), so the toolhead
crosses open air on every layer and unretracted ooze shows as strings in the
gap. Generated by `make_stringing_test.py` (checked in beside this record).

Bands are 25 layers = 5 mm of height each:

| Z (mm) | Layers | Retraction length | Retraction speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | 1–25 | 0.5 mm | 15 mm/s (baseline) |
| 5–10 | 26–50 | 0.5 mm | 35 mm/s |
| 10–15 | 51–75 | 0.8 mm | 35 mm/s |
| 15–20 | 76–100 | 1.1 mm | 35 mm/s |
| 20–25 | 101–125 | 1.4 mm | 35 mm/s |
| 25–30 | 126–150 | 1.8 mm | 35 mm/s |

Band 1 vs band 2 isolates retraction **speed**; bands 2→6 sweep **length** at
the faster speed.

### How the file was built

OrcaSlicer's own Retraction test lives in the GUI, and the CLI has no
calibration mode, so the sweep was produced deterministically instead:

1. `make_stringing_test.py` writes the two-pillar STL.
2. `OrcaSlicer --arrange 1 --export-3mf` turns the STL into a project, then the
   embedded `Metadata/project_settings.config` is replaced with the Loom
   project settings from `../2026-09-14-T1/loom_T1.3mf`, patched to 205/210 °C
   with a 3 mm outer brim. (Orca's CLI rejects `--load-settings` on a project
   config — `from: project` is unsupported — and errors on unknown config keys
   passed as flags, so patching the 3mf is the workable path.)
3. `OrcaSlicer --arrange 1 --slice 0` produces `plate_1.gcode`.
4. `band_retraction.py` rewrites each retract/deretract **pair** per band.

`band_retraction.py` pairs each `G1 E-<len> F<speed>` with its partner and
assigns the band from the **retract**, so both halves always get the same
value and net extrusion is unchanged. Verified on the generated file:

- every line that is not a retraction is byte-identical to the slice;
- retraction net E is −1.8 mm in the banded file vs −0.5 mm in the slice —
  exactly the final unmatched retract at end of print, i.e. all pairs balance;
- 601 retractions rewritten, ~100 per band.

This works because the profile uses relative E, software retraction and no
wipe, which makes every retraction a bare E-only move. `band_retraction.py`
fails closed if that assumption breaks (unmatched retract or deretraction).

## Results

`USER-OBSERVED` from owner photos (NAS `originals/incoming/20260915/`,
`IMG_4126`, `IMG_4135`–`IMG_4138`; band-annotated crops in this folder,
`analysis_bands_IMG_4138.jpg`, `analysis_bands_IMG_4136.jpg`,
`analysis_gap_IMG_4135.jpg`). Band boundaries were located from each photo's
row-brightness profile, not eyeballed.

**The 52 mm gap between the pillars is clean at every band** (`IMG_4135`). No
taut strings bridge the pillars anywhere up the height, including band 1 — the
0.5 mm @ 15 mm/s **baseline**. This is the direct contrast with 2026-09-14,
where the temperature tower webbed at every band. So a retraction of the
profile's current length, on this geometry, already suppresses the gap
stringing that the tower showed; the sweep did not reveal a "first clean" band
because band 1 was already clean.

What the sweep **does** show, on the pillar faces and tops:

- **Bands 1–4 (0.5–1.1 mm): clean, even faces.** Band 1 vs band 2 (speed
  15 → 35 mm/s at the same 0.5 mm) is not visibly distinguishable in the gap or
  on the faces — retraction **speed** made no read-off difference here.
- **Bands 5–6 (1.4 mm, 1.8 mm): mild over-retraction artefacts.** The upper
  faces are slightly rougher/pocked and the coarse stray hairs concentrate
  toward the top. Longer retraction bought nothing and started to cost surface
  quality.
- **A few coarse stray filaments** hang off the pillar tops/corners (clearest
  in `IMG_4136`/`IMG_4138`) and one small blob sits near the band-1/2 boundary
  of one pillar (`IMG_4138`, ~Z5). These are travel/seam stragglers and a small
  ooze knot, not dense webbing — wipe and seam/pressure-advance territory, not
  retraction length.

**Recommendation.** Keep retraction short: **0.5 mm, speed raised to 35 mm/s**
(harmless, and it matches the rest of the profile's fast moves), or 0.8 mm if a
margin is wanted — do **not** go to 1.4–1.8 mm. To chase the residual stray
hairs, enable **wipe** (currently off) and revisit pressure advance
(`extruder1 ADVANCE=0.03`) before touching retraction further.

**Caveat on the test.** The two-pillar / 52 mm-travel geometry is *less*
demanding than the tower's tight seam travels: it under-discriminates because
even the baseline passed. A finer ranking would need closer pillars or a
thin-spike stringing model — but since the part is already clean, that is
probably not worth a print. Treat the numbers above as "good enough, don't
over-retract," not as a finely-resolved optimum.

- `SAVE_VARIABLE printing_info_auto_leveling 0 → 1` (fresh probe for this run).
- No other variable changed; the 2026-09-14 offsets (`x −32.823188,
  y −0.006438, z 2.57`) and preheat values were already correct and were left
  untouched. No `SAVE_CONFIG` was issued by this session, and
  `[tension_sensor] trigger_sample_count = 6` is still pending and unwritten.
- **`printer.cfg` nevertheless changed during the print.** Read at 09:17 it was
  9,545 bytes (sha256 `f8eb0943…`); read at 10:11 it was 9,541 bytes (sha256
  `33322fd4…`, LM-022). The sole difference is the `[bed_mesh runtime_active]`
  block, replaced with this morning's fresh probe; `Last-Modified` is 09:50,
  when the probe finished. `INFERENCE`: the vendor mesh routine writes
  `printer.cfg` itself so a fresh mesh survives reboot — Klipper still reports
  `save_config_pending: true` listing that same block, so this is not Klipper's
  own `SAVE_CONFIG` path. Anything that rewrites `printer.cfg` by hand must
  therefore re-read it immediately beforehand. (Later the same day the vendor
  macro comment in `calibration.cfg`, LM-025, confirmed the mechanism: a mesh
  profile load runs `save_runtime_mesh_snapshot` → `SAVE_CONFIG R=0`.)

## Deferred: `[firmware_retraction]`

The intended approach was to add `[firmware_retraction]` to `printer.cfg` so a
print could sweep retraction with `SET_RETRACTION` and so retraction could be
retuned mid-print without reslicing. **Blocked, not abandoned:**

- Moonraker exposes the `config` root **read-only** (`/server/files/roots` →
  `config: "r"`), so `printer.cfg` cannot be written over the API.
- SSH to `anisoprint@<printer-ip>` refuses public-key auth; no key is
  installed and the owner does not hold a password. The owner emailed vendor
  support 2026-09-15 to request shell credentials; outcome uncertain.

The vendor Klipper fork **does** ship the module
(`_zipcrypto_work/ex2/klipper_2.2.42/klippy/extras/firmware_retraction.py`,
stock upstream), and nothing in the live config defines it or collides with
G10/G11 — the only references are the standard Mainsail pause macro's
`use_fw_retract` guard, which defaults false. The prepared block is a
nine-line addition after `[input_shaper]` with defaults matching the current
Orca values, so behaviour would be unchanged until `SET_RETRACTION` is issued.

`CAUTION` if this is adopted: the module's `SET_RETRACTION` clears its
`is_retracted` flag, so issuing it **between** a G10 and its G11 silently
swallows that unretract. Any future firmware-retraction sweep must change
parameters only while unretracted.
