Also `CONFIRMED`:
- **Tension sensor** (LM-016): 18 baseline jam checks between 22:43 and 23:47,
  all within limit. Deviation ranged −3,055 to +2,737 against
  `jam_deviation_limit` 55,000. No jam event.
- **AI detector** (LM-019): 1,095 `analysis_result` lines during the job, all
  "0 records IOU ≥ 0.2 (4 required)". The detector never met its trigger.

Interpretation:
- `INFERENCE`: the CFC-plastic feed into the extruder was not stalled in
  section #2. Its encoder ratio (92%) equals the later sections. The encoder
  resolution of about 1–2 mm per event accounts for the sub-100% ratios. So
  the symptom was at the nozzle outlet, not upstream.
- `INFERENCE`, supporting the owner's hypothesis: #2 followed the longest
  standby of the job, 10.4 min at 150 °C against about 2.8 min for #4–#17. The
  heat-up time and the fibre start after 270 °C were not different for #2. A
  longer standby lets CFC PETG ooze and degrade on the tip; heat-degraded PETG
  yellows. The first fibre section then drags that residue into a clump. The
  pause (80 s held at 270 °C) and the resume may have cleared it. Moonraker's
  `u_filament_used` rose from 586.2 to 641.2 mm across the resume (LM-017),
  consistent with a 55 mm fibre restart extrusion. No later section
  followed a long standby, and no recurrence was reported.
- `OPEN QUESTION`: whether the clump was CFC PETG, degraded residue, or fibre
  resin. It was not sampled.

## 8. Corrections to the protocol

`TEST_PRINT_PROTOCOL.md` §3.4 said Loom pauses on "Chamber door is open".
LM-017 shows door-open pause is **disabled** on Loom at this time. The pause
message exists in the configuration, but the pre-check skips it.
