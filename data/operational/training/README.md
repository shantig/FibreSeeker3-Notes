# Official Training Video Inventory

Seven video lesson pages were recovered from the official Anisoprint training
curriculum. Each record preserves the canonical Anisoprint page, page source
ID, Vimeo ID/URL, title, course, publication date, duration, relevance tags,
written-document relationships, and transcript state.

All seven are `READY_FOR_HUMAN_NOTES`. No public caption or transcript asset was
exposed in the acquired Anisoprint HTML, so every record is
`NOT_PUBLICLY_EXPOSED`. This status is an unresolved acquisition result, not a
claim that captions never existed.

A future note such as `VID-AP-124 @ 12:43` must be stored as
`HUMAN_TIMESTAMP_NOTE` with `UNVERIFIED_NOTE` guidance status and
`UNRESOLVED` FibreSeek applicability until independently sourced. The fixture
`2FA-08` enforces that boundary.
